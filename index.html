The Problem

Pets are invisible
to the digital world.

Millions of pets suffer because their medical history, identity, and ownership are locked in paper files or scattered across disconnected systems.

🔍
Lost & Unidentified Pets
Without a universal digital identity, lost pets often can't be traced back to their owners, leading to heartbreak and shelter overcrowding.
2.4M / yr
📋
Fragmented Medical Records
Vaccination records, vet reports, and prescriptions exist on paper across multiple clinics — impossible to access in emergencies.
68% paper-only
🏥
Emergency Delays
Vets waste critical time gathering basic information. No centralized profile means guessing allergies, medications, and medical history.
~45 min avg delay
🔐
No Ownership Verification
Pets are bought and sold without blockchain-verified ownership records, enabling adoption fraud and illegal breeding operations.
$1.2B fraud market
⏰
Missed Health Reminders
Without automated systems, pet owners forget critical vaccinations, deworming schedules, and annual checkups that prevent disease.
3 in 5 pets
🌐
No Centralized Platform
The pet industry is fragmented across 40,000+ clinics, shelters, and registries — none connected, none speaking to each other.
Zero interop

AI + Automation

Intelligent by
design.

PetOlife isn't just a database — it's an AI-native platform that thinks, learns, and acts on behalf of every pet and owner.

  • OpenAI GPT-4o generates unique AI pet identity summaries on registration
  • AI symptom checker analyzes inputs and flags risk levels in real-time
  • n8n automation orchestrates registration → QR → email → reminder workflows
  • WhatsApp Business API sends vaccination reminders 7, 3, and 1 day before due dates
  • Scheduled cron jobs auto-trigger checkup reminders based on breed age profiles
  • MongoDB Atlas vector search enables semantic pet health queries

Built for the
future of petcare.

Verified Digital ID

🤖
